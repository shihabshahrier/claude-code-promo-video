# Worked example — the LetX 30s promo

> The finished scene sheet, voiceover script and full cue list for the video
> at <https://youtube.com/shorts/CPx11OL0yMc>. This is what step 1 and step 3
> of the prompts produce once you've iterated on them.

1080×1920 · 30 fps · 30.000 s · 9:16 for Reels / Shorts / TikTok
Safe zone: y 260–1560, x 70–950.

Scene cuts: **3.0 / 8.0 / 19.0 / 25.0** — every audio cue is keyed to these.
Score is 120 BPM (beat 0.5 s, bar 2.0 s), Am–F–C–G, one chord per bar, so the
4-bar loop is exactly 8.0 s and lands on the cuts.

## Mix decisions

- Music sits at **0.30** gain and is **ducked -11.5 dB** under the VO, with 50 ms
  lookahead so the duck opens *before* the word, not on top of it.
- A **-3.4 dB dip at 2.4 kHz** is carved into the music so speech has a pocket.
- SFX ducked only **-4.5 dB** — impacts should still hit through the VO.
- Sub is boosted +2 dB at 55 Hz. Most of this plays on phone speakers, but it
  matters on headphones and it's most of the "premium" impression.
- Master: **-14.0 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP**. Peak measured -1.4 dBFS.
