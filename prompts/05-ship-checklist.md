# Step 5 — Check it, then ship

**Where:** You  
**Time:** ~10 min

Two checks catch almost every embarrassing mistake: watch it once muted, and listen to it once on a phone speaker. Then verify every number on screen is one you can defend.

**You end up with:** A file you are willing to put your product’s name on.

---

```
WATCH IT MUTED.
  Does the story still work with no sound? If the video only makes
  sense with narration, the visuals are not carrying their weight —
  and most people scroll with the sound off.

LISTEN ON A PHONE SPEAKER.
  Not headphones. Phone speakers have no bass, so if the mix depends
  on sub to feel big, it will sound thin where it actually plays.

CHECK THE FIRST 2 SECONDS.
  Cover the rest. Would you stop scrolling? Be honest. This is the
  only part most viewers will ever see.

CHECK THE LAST FRAME.
  Pause on it. It becomes the thumbnail. Is the product name legible?
  Is there a URL?

VERIFY EVERY NUMBER.
  Every stat on screen must trace to something you can show. Round
  down, never up — an "N+" claim never expires as you grow. If you
  cannot verify it, cut it or route the script around it.

ASK CLAUDE CODE:
  "Play back the final render and tell me the measured LUFS, the true
  peak, and whether any voiceover line collides with the line after
  it or with an impact cue."
```
